# Screenshots

Promo shots of **Springfield Battle Royale**.

## How to take one

While playing:

| Key | What you get |
|---|---|
| **P** | Screenshot **with** the HUD — health, ammo, minimap, kill feed |
| **Shift + P** | **Clean** screenshot, no HUD at all |

Shots save straight to your **Downloads** folder, named by date and time
(`springfield-clean-2026-08-15_17-01-30.png`) so they sort in order.

Drag the keepers into this folder to keep them together.

## Which to use

- **Shift + P (clean)** — posters, thumbnails, showing the game to people.
  No numbers cluttering the picture, just Springfield.
- **P (with HUD)** — proving something happened. A victory screen, a
  legendary weapon, "1 ALIVE" in the corner.

## Good shots to try

- The **VICTORY!** screen after a win (use plain **P** so the placement
  and elimination count are visible — that's the whole point of the shot)
- Standing in front of the **donut shop** with the giant donut behind you
- **Crouched** behind a fence holding a Legendary Shotgun, big round crosshair
- **Skydiving** in at the very start, town spread out below
- The **pink storm** wall closing in, glowing across the map
- A **gold chest** just as you open it
